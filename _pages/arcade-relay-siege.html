Relay Siege

Defend the relay for six waves. Place towers to lengthen the enemy route and cover as much of it as possible.

Towers and controls
  • Lasers fire quickly. Stasis towers slow enemies. Railguns fire more slowly but ignore armor and reach farther.
  • Place a tower on an empty tile and leave a path to the relay. Select a built tower to change its target priority, upgrade it or salvage it for 60% of its cost.
  • Armored enemies take reduced laser damage and cost two integrity if they escape. A cleared wave grants 35 credits.
