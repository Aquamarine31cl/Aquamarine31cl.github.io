Relay Siege

Defend the relay for six waves. Build beside the dotted route and keep a path open from the entrance.

Controls and rules
  • Lasers fire quickly, stasis towers slow enemies, and railguns reach farther and ignore armor.
  • Select a built tower to set target priority or upgrade it. Salvage refunds 60% of the tower and upgrade costs.
  • Armored enemies cost two integrity if they escape. Clearing a wave grants 35 credits.
