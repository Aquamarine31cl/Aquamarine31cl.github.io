Relay Siege

Protect the relay through six waves. Place laser towers (35 credits) for damage and stasis towers (50) to slow enemies. Towers block movement, so every placement reshapes the shortest route. You must leave a route open. Armored enemies take reduced laser damage and cost two integrity if they escape.

Select a tile, then Build. Upgrade the selected tower to level 3 for 30 and 60 credits, or salvage it for 60% of its total investment. Each kill funds more construction. A cleared wave grants 35 credits. On narrow screens, scroll the board sideways. Arrow keys navigate tiles.
