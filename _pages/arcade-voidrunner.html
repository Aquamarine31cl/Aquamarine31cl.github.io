Voidrunner

Clear four waves. Take cover behind asteroids and use Pulse to clear nearby enemy shots.

Controls and rules
  • W thrusts, A and D turn, S brakes, Space fires, E pulses and P pauses. Arrow keys also steer and thrust. On touch screens, hold the controls below the flight view.
  • Pulse clears shots within 110 units and recharges in 8 seconds. Shields start recharging after 4 seconds without damage.
  • Pink raiders circle, gold interceptors close in, and violet gunships keep their distance. Each cleared wave gives one upgrade and repairs 30 hull.
  • Practice mode spaces out arrivals and reduces damage. Retry Wave restores your ship and score to the start of that wave.

Ready. Select Launch to begin.
