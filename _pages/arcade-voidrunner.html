Voidrunner

Clear four waves. Keep momentum while firing, use asteroids as cover, and let your weapon cool between bursts. Shields regenerate after four seconds without damage. Each cleared sector grants one upgrade and repairs 30 hull.

Keyboard: W/↑ thrust, A/← and D/→ steer, S/↓ or Shift brake, Space fire, P pause. Focus the flight view to use keyboard controls. On touch screens, hold the controls below. The flight pauses when you leave it.

Ready. Select Launch to begin.
