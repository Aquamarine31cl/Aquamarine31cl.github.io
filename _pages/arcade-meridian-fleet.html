Meridian Fleet

Win within 18 rounds by earning 8 relay points or destroying the enemy fleet. Select a friendly ship, then choose a destination or target.

Controls and rules
  • Ships have 4 action points per round. Movement costs 1 per tile and Fire costs 2. Asteroids block both movement and firing lines.
  • Each uncontested golden relay grants 1 point per round when a friendly ship is within one tile. Side and rear shots bypass shields and deal 25% more hull damage.
  • Missile costs 3 points and hits the selected tile and its four neighbors after the enemy response. It can hit allies. Red tiles mark the blast area.
  • Brace restores shields, cools the ship and turns it toward the target. Overwatch fires once when an enemy moves into range. Both cost 2 points.
  • Enemies contest relays and avoid missile blasts. Reinforcements arrive in rounds 6 and 10. You can undo orders before ending the turn.
