Helios Accord

A 36-cycle colony campaign. Build a connected industrial network, balance power and heat, survive solar flares, and launch three navigation beacons. Each cycle allows one construction, repair, research or launch order. You can inspect the board without spending that order.

Colony Manual & Module Reference
  • Select a hex sector, then issue an order. New modules must touch the connected colony. Relays bridge sectors and add 20 battery capacity per level.
  • Core production: 8 energy and 4 alloy per cycle. Extractors consume 3 power per level and mine finite deposits; labs consume 5 power per level and produce 3 science per level. Power prioritizes cooling and shields. Unpowered or disconnected modules do nothing.
  • Solar output changes with the six sun sectors every three cycles. Adjacent arrays shade each other. Flares every six cycles halve solar output and damage unshielded modules.
  • Heat above 24 damages modules. Radiators cool themselves and adjacent modules. Shields protect their own hex and adjacent hexes. Upgrade modules to level 3, research cooling, efficiency or armor to level 2, or salvage 60% of a module's base cost.
  • Research costs 15 alloy and 12 / 24 science. Beacons cost 38 / 50 / 62 alloy, 18 / 26 / 34 science, and 20 energy each. Build an extractor and lab early; protect the core before the first flare.
