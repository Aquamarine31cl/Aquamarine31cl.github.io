Helios Accord

Launch three beacons within 36 cycles. Build an extractor for alloy and a lab for science, then check the forecast before advancing.

Controls and rules
  • Build on an empty hex next to the connected colony. Each cycle allows one order. The core produces 8 energy and 4 alloy per cycle.
  • Extractors use finite deposits. Solar output varies with the sun and drops during flares. Relays connect sectors and add 20 battery capacity per level.
  • Radiators cool adjacent modules. Shields protect their hex and its neighbors. Orange borders mark projected damage next cycle.
  • Launch Beacon displays its current cost. You can undo the last order before advancing.
