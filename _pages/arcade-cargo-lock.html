Cargo Lock

Push every crate onto a marked bay. Move beside a crate to push it one square.

Controls and rules
  • Crates can only be pushed, one at a time. A crate pushed against two walls can get stuck.
  • Use the on-screen arrows, tap an adjacent tile or press the arrow keys or WASD. Undo restores the previous turn.
