Signal Lattice

Connect every tile to the power source at the top left. Tap a tile to rotate it clockwise.

Controls and rules
  • Powered tiles light up. A connection only counts if the port on the adjacent tile faces it.
  • Use the arrow keys to select a tile and Enter to rotate it.
