Kepler Courier

Deliver cargo to three stations. To dock, get within 15 units and slow to less than 1.8 units per second relative to the station.

Controls and rules
  • W or Space thrusts, A and D turn, and Shift gives quarter-power thrust. Q points along your orbit, E points against it, and P pauses.
  • To raise your orbit, thrust prograde, coast to the higher altitude, then thrust prograde again to circularize. Retrograde thrust lowers your orbit. Use the dashed trajectory and arrival offset to plan the transfer.
  • Within 70 units, R or Align for Docking points the ship in the direction of a velocity correction. Use short burns and return to 1× speed near the station.
  • Keep periapsis above 65 and stay inside radius 650. Retry Delivery restores the start of the current leg.
