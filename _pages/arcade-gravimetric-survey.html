Gravimetric Survey

Scan every safe square. Numbers count hazards in the eight surrounding squares.

Controls and rules
  • Your first scan and the surrounding squares are safe. Flag a suspected hazard using Flag Mode, right-click or the F key.
  • Scan a revealed number again to open its unflagged neighbors once enough flags surround it.
  • Check a Clue can highlight a move deduced from the numbers, assuming your flags are correct.
