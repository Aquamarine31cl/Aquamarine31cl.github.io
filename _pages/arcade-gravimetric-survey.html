Hazard deduction

Gravimetric Survey

Reveal every safe sector. Numbers count nearby hazards, including diagonals. Your first scan and its neighbors are safe. Scan an already revealed number to open its unflagged neighbors when the adjacent flag count matches.

Arrows / WASD select. Enter or Space scans. F or right-click flags. On touch, turn on Flag Mode and tap a sector. Z undoes an action.
