Shadow Dock

Take the key and reach the exit. Check the amber outline before moving into the guard’s next sightline.

Controls and rules
  • Pink shows the current sightline. The guard advances once after each move or wait.
  • Tap the arrows or use the arrow keys or WASD. Use Wait or Space to let the guard move first.
