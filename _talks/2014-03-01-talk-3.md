The 3rd International Conference on Molecular Engineering of Polymers (MEP-3) was held from September 17 to 21, 2026, in Shanghai, China. Organized by the State Key Laboratory of Molecular Engineering of Polymers and the Department of Macromolecular Science at Fudan University, the conference was part of a biennial series that began with MEP-1 in 2016 and MEP-2 in 2018, both also held in Shanghai.
The program covered four main themes: synthesis, structure and function of polymers; commodity polymers and polymer physics; biomedical materials; and energy-related materials. The conference brought together leading scientists and young researchers from Europe, the Americas, Asia, and China to discuss frontier developments and emerging applications in polymer science. 
The scientific program included plenary lectures, invited talks, oral presentations, and poster sessions, with a Young Scientists Forum held alongside poster presentations. Ten best poster awards were granted. The official language of the conference was English. Key deadlines included August 30, 2026 for abstract submission and September 7, 2026 for early-bird registration. The conference took place at the Sheraton Shanghai Waigaoqiao Hotel.
