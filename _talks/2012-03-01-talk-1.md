Held October 25, 2025, at BIT Liangxiang Campus. Guided by the Joint Committee of Honors Colleges of the Excellence League (E9); hosted by Xuteli College / School of Future Technologies / Excellence Engineer College / Teli College, BIT; co-organized by honors colleges of eight other E9 universities. Theme: “Innovation Inspires Wisdom…” Program: keynote lectures, three sub-forums (Mechanical and Vehicle Engineering; Information and Electronics; Computer and AI), 35 student reports. Over 1,000 participants.
