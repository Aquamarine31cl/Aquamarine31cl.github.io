The First E9 League Honors College Outstanding Students Academic Exchange Conference & the 8th Academic Forum of Xuteli College was held on October 25, 2025, at the Liangxiang Campus of Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT) in Beijing, China. The event was guided by the E9 League Honors College Joint Conference and hosted by BIT’s Xuteli College / Future Precision Technology College / Excellence Engineer College / Xuteli Residential College, with co-organization by honors colleges from eight other E9 League universities, including Chongqing University, Dalian University of Technology, Southeast University, Harbin Institute of Technology, South China University of Technology, Tianjin University, Tongji University, and Northwestern Polytechnical University.
Under the theme “Innovation Inspires Wisdom, Deep Scholarship, Excellence Reaches Far, Co-creating the Future,” the conference aimed to broaden students’ scientific horizons, enhance research literacy and innovative practice capabilities, and build a cross-disciplinary platform for academic exchange among outstanding students from E9 honors colleges. The program featured an opening ceremony, a main forum with keynote lectures by professors Shen Jun and Yang Yi, and three parallel sub-forums: “Mechanical and Vehicle Engineering,” “Information and Electronics,” and “Computer and Artificial Intelligence”. A total of 35 student representatives presented their research in the sub-forums.
Over 1,000 participants attended, including guests from E9 universities, BIT faculty and administrators, and experts, teachers, and students from E9 honors colleges. During the opening ceremony, nine faculty members received the “Outstanding Academic Mentor” certificate, and 16 undergraduates were awarded the “Outstanding Undergraduate Academic Performance Award”. The conference provided a platform for young scholars to showcase their talents, exchange ideas, and contribute to the cultivation of top-tier innovative talent through deepened inter-university cooperation among E9 honors colleges.
