Held July 26–31, 2026, at Lanzhou University. Sponsored by the Professional Committee on Chiral Chemistry, Chinese Chemical Society; organized by the State Key Laboratory of Applied Organic Chemistry and the College of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Lanzhou University. Ten experts lectured, including four CAS academicians (Feng Xiaoming, You Shuli, Ding Kuiling, Wang Meixiang). Topics: asymmetric catalysis, supramolecular chirality, chiral polymers, chiral porous materials. Over 300 participants from 50+ institutions; certificates upon completion.
