The 14th Chiral Matter Science Summer School (Chiral Matter Science Summer School 2026) was held from July 26 to 31, 2026, at Lanzhou University in Lanzhou, China. Sponsored by the Professional Committee on Chiral Chemistry of the Chinese Chemical Society and organized by the State Key Laboratory of Applied Organic Chemistry at Lanzhou University, the school is a national academic training program that has been held annually since 2012 .
The program featured lectures by ten distinguished experts, including four academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences: Feng Xiaoming, You Shuli, Ding Kuiling, and Wang Meixiang. Course topics covered frontiers in chiral science, such as asymmetric catalysis, supramolecular chirality, chiral polymers, and chiral porous materials. The curriculum combined lectures with seminars to emphasize both foundational theory and cutting-edge research .
Over 300 graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and young faculty from more than 50 universities and research institutes across China participated. The school provided free accommodation, meals, and study materials for admitted participants, and those who completed the program received a certificate of completion .
