The inaugural Guohao Undergraduate Academic Forum was held on Tongji University's Siping Road Campus on 22–23 November 2025. Led by Guohao College, the event created a platform for undergraduates to share research and connect across disciplines. Its focus on intelligent engineering innovation linked student inquiry with emerging engineering challenges. The programme brought together academic speakers and student participants, including Chinese Academy of Engineering academician Hehua Zhu.
