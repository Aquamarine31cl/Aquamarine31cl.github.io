Held November 22–23, 2025, at Tongji University Siping Road Campus. Spearheaded by Guohao College, Tongji University, under the guidance of the E9 Honors College Joint Conference and several Tongji departments. Theme: “Intelligent Engineering Innovation.” Program: keynote by CAE academician Zhu Hehua, four sub-forums (AI Frontiers; Interdisciplinary Innovation; Digital Intelligence Engineering; Green Energy and Urban Development), poster session with awards. Over 15 universities participated.
