Held September 17–21, 2026, in Shanghai. Organized by the State Key Laboratory of Molecular Engineering of Polymers and the Department of Macromolecular Science, Fudan University. The biennial series began with MEP-1 (2016) and MEP-2 (2018). Themes: polymer synthesis, structure and function; commodity polymers and polymer physics; biomedical materials; energy-related materials. Program: plenary and invited lectures, oral and poster sessions, Young Scientists Forum, ten best poster awards. Official language: English.
