Organized by Fudan University's National Key Laboratory of Molecular Engineering of Polymers and Department of Macromolecular Science, MEP-2026 brought researchers from 21 countries and regions to Shanghai. Scientific sessions on 18–20 September covered polymer synthesis, structure and properties; commodity polymers and polymer physics; biomedical materials and applications; and energy-related materials and technologies. Plenary and invited lectures, oral presentations, a Young Scientists Forum and poster discussions supported exchange across these areas. The conference website advertised the broader event window as 17–21 September, including the surrounding programme.
