Held at the Wenbo Center on BIT's Liangxiang Campus, this forum brought together honors colleges across the Excellence League (E9) for student research exchange. Hosted by BIT's Xuteli College and partner colleges under the E9 honors-college joint committee, the programme combined invited lectures with three parallel tracks: mechanical and vehicle engineering, information and electronics, and computer science and artificial intelligence. The event provided a platform for students to exchange research ideas and connect across disciplines.
