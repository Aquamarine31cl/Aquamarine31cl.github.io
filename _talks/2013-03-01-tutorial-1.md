The First “Guohao Forum” Undergraduate Academic Forum of Tongji University was held from November 22 to 23, 2025, at the Siping Road Campus of Tongji University in Shanghai, China. The forum was spearheaded by Guohao School of Tongji University (also rendered as Guohao College), under the guidance of the E9 League Honors College Joint Conference, Tongji University’s Party Committee Student Work Department, Undergraduate School, Innovation and Entrepreneurship School, and the Communist Youth League Committee.

Under the theme “Intelligent Engineering Innovation,” the forum aimed to build an interdisciplinary academic exchange platform for undergraduates, foster a strong academic atmosphere, and encourage students to pursue academic excellence in service of the country. The program featured an opening ceremony, a main forum with keynote lectures by Academician Zhu Hehua of the Chinese Academy of Engineering and researcher Feng Kairui, student representative reports, and four parallel sub-forums: “AI Frontiers and Applications,” “Interdisciplinary Innovation in Basic Disciplines,” “Digital Intelligence Engineering and Advanced Manufacturing,” and “Green Energy and Urban Development”. A poster presentation session was also held, with awards including Best Creativity, Best Presentation, and Best Practice selected by audience vote.

Over 15 universities participated, including Zhejiang University, Beijing Institute of Technology, and Xi’an Jiaotong University, alongside faculty and students from Tongji University. The forum served as a platform for undergraduates to showcase research, exchange ideas across disciplines, and strengthen inter-university cooperation among E9 honors colleges.
